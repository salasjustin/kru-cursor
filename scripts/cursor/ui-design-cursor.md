
**External designer on Cursor.** Mockups and directions are made outside this engineering team. On Cursor, the designer bot loads `skills/kru-design/SKILL.md` and publishes with `/canvas` ([canvas](https://cursor.com/docs/agent/tools/canvas)); `.dc.html` artboards are the fallback when `/canvas` isn't available. The PM bot attaches the approved mockups and build spec to the brief (`references/design-spec.md`, `references/designer-handoff.md`). Engineering never drafts mockups or runs a design step. A `.design-sync/` bundle, if present, is for the designer — Cursor has no claude.ai/design host for that lane (`references/design-sync.md` records upstream; engineering does not run it).
