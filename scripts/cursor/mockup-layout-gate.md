- **Mockup layout gate (before the PM).** Whenever `kru-ui-designer` or `/kru-design` returns artboards or a canvas publish, those mockups pass **`kru-visual-reviewer`** before the PM (or owner) sees the card or paths. **`kru-ui-designer` already runs `composition` → *The pass* on every artboard before publish**; that self-check is not the gate — the reviewer **renders and measures independently** and the drafter does not grade its own work. This is not the design verdict (that stays the PM's eye, below); it applies the **same composition relations** the pass checked — entries in `skills/kru-composition/reference/layout.md` (and `hierarchy.md` / `text.md` where the brief loads them) — with **selector or `file:line` and measured px**. **Existing-system repos too:** skipping the design hop for a build does not skip this gate when `kru-ui-designer` or `/kru-design` produces a new page or screen mockup.
  1. **Render.** Open each artboard in a real browser at **its stated width only** — desktop artboards at **1440**, mobile at **375** (the seat loads the **local-browser** skill → `emulate`); never open a desktop file at 375. Targets are each `.dc.html` URL the lead serves at that width — not a `/canvas` card (the canvas card and paths stay withheld until step 4). The lead serves `.design-work/` before dispatch — not only when `file://` blocks fonts or images: `python3 -m http.server 4400 -d .design-work` from the repo root, then hands the reviewer `http://localhost:4400/…` — **`kru-visual-reviewer` still never starts a server**; if nothing answers, it returns that gap and the lead fixes reachability before re-dispatch. Mockup layout is **exempt** from the Step 4 dev-server requirement in the **visual-review** skill (the body `kru-visual-reviewer` loads) and satisfies the **local-browser** skill's localhost rule via that URL.
  2. **Brief mockup layout.** Hand `kru-visual-reviewer` the artboard list, each file's viewport, and **mockup layout** intent: verify the pass's **done-when** (sibling-group gaps are strictly larger space-ladder steps than any gap inside the group; section headings satisfy `layout.md` → *The space around a group is larger than the space inside it*) and **step 5** — every loaded `composition` entry, cited by section name. The mockup brief **overrides** that skill's *What this pass is for* "user already looked" and *Numbers are evidence* "no computed-value sweep" limits; the sweep measures **composition entries only**. Findings carry **the entry violated**, **selector or `file:line`**, and **measured px** — not taste. Proof screenshots are not in scope for the lead's Proof block.
  3. **Loop.** **FIX** → brief `kru-ui-designer` to redraw from the on-disk artboards and re-publish if needed → re-run the reviewer. **Cap at three reviewer passes** — the named exception to *Max 2 loops per seat* below; a third layout-gate pass is not a bad-brief signal. After the cap, show the PM the draft **and** any open layout findings — do not block on perfection, but do not hide the list.
  4. **Then the PM channel.** Only after step 3 (clean **SHIP** on layout, or pass 3 exhausted) hand the canvas card or `.dc.html` paths to the PM with the seat's design question. The PM's eye on the render remains the design gate; this gate only keeps failed composition checks from reaching them cold.
