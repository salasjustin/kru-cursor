- **Mockup layout gate (before the PM).** Every screen mockup — a `.dc.html` artboard, a `/canvas` publish, or any rendered preview the lead would put in front of the PM — passes **`kru-visual-reviewer`** before the PM (or owner) sees it. **`kru-ui-designer` already runs `composition` → *The pass* on every artboard before publish**; that self-check is not the gate — the reviewer **renders and measures independently** and the drafter does not grade its own work. This is not the design verdict (that stays the PM's eye, below); it applies the **same composition relations** the pass checked — entries in `skills/kru-composition/reference/layout.md` (and `hierarchy.md` / `text.md` where the brief loads them) — with **selector or `file:line` and measured px**. **Existing-system repos too:** skipping the design hop for a build does not skip this gate when `kru-ui-designer` or `/kru-design` produces a new page or screen mockup.
  1. **Render.** Open each artboard in a real browser at **desktop 1440** and **mobile 375** (`local-browser` → `emulate`). Targets are the absolute `.dc.html` paths or the canvas URL. The lead serves `.design-work/` read-only when `file://` blocks fonts or images; **`kru-visual-reviewer` still never starts a server** — if nothing answers, it returns that gap and the lead fixes reachability before re-dispatch.
  2. **Brief mockup layout.** Hand `kru-visual-reviewer` the artboard list, both viewports, and **mockup layout** intent: verify the pass's **done-when** (every node's sibling gap > any internal gap) and **step 5** — every loaded `composition` entry, cited by section name. Findings carry **the entry violated**, **selector or `file:line`**, and **measured px** — not taste.
  3. **Loop.** **FIX** → brief `kru-ui-designer` to redraw from the on-disk artboards and re-publish if needed → re-run the reviewer. **Cap at three rounds.** After the cap, show the PM the draft **and** any open layout findings — do not block on perfection, but do not hide the list.
  4. **Then the PM channel.** Only after step 3 (clean **SHIP** on layout, or round 3 exhausted) hand the canvas card or `.dc.html` paths to the PM with the seat's design question. The PM's eye on the render remains the design gate; this gate only keeps failed composition checks from reaching them cold.
